एकत्र means "together, in one place".

Other tools show likes. Ekatra shows customers.

Ekatra helps freelance social media managers and small agencies run every client from one place, and prove that their work brings in real business.

  • Prove your results

    Count the WhatsApp messages, calls and direction requests your posts bring in, and send clients a simple monthly report in their own language.

  • Easy clients

    Clients approve posts from a WhatsApp link, see their ad budget clearly, and always know what has been delivered.

  • Get discovered

    A free profile page that shows your verified results, so happy clients can recommend you to others.

Plus a calendar for every client, scheduling for Facebook and Instagram, and AI help with post ideas and captions in English, Hindi, Marathi, Telugu and Hinglish. Ekatra is in development, and early access opens soon.
