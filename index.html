About us

Start with one. Do it well. Then grow.

Shubpra Group was started in 2026 by two engineers who studied together in college.

We want to build a group of businesses that solve everyday problems for Indian customers, in software, services and manufacturing. Instead of doing everything at once, we're starting with one venture, Ekatra, and giving it our full attention.

Everything we make should be simple to understand, fairly priced, and built around how people in India actually live and work.
